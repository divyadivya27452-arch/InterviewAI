How to Use Interview AI

Follow these four simple steps to practice and track measurable progress.

1

Configure Session

Pick your target role, difficulty (Beginner to Advanced), question type (Technical, HR, or Mixed), and length (5, 10, or 20 questions).

2

Answer via Text or Voice

Type your response or click Voice Input to dictate with your microphone using your browser's Web Speech API.

3

Review AI Feedback

After submitting, inspect your 0–100 score, 4-skill breakdown, strengths, weaknesses, and model answer hints per question.

4

Track Growth

Visit Dashboard and Performance Analytics to watch your score trends, role averages, and improved skills climb over time.
